Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version: Your scouting data stays on your device. We don't collect it, store it on a server, or share it. blitzIQ has no user accounts and no tracking.

What we collect

Your scouting data (opponents, snaps, formations, calls): stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else. We cannot see it. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, it's gone — use the in-app Export Backup to keep a copy.

We do not collect names, emails, analytics, cookies, location, or usage tracking through the app itself.

Third-party services

  • Software libraries (CDN): the app loads open-source libraries (e.g. PDF and spreadsheet readers) from a content-delivery network so it can read your Hudl files. These requests are standard web traffic and do not include your scouting data.
  • Payments (Gumroad): if you buy a Season Pass, payment is handled by Gumroad. We never see your card details. Gumroad's handling of your information is governed by Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • License verification: when you activate a Season Pass, the app sends your license key to Gumroad to confirm the purchase. No scouting data is sent.

Children's privacy

blitzIQ is a tool for coaches and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
