Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: Your scouting data — every opponent, snap, formation and call — stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. DefWise has no accounts and no logins. We do use a product-analytics tool to see which features get used, and if you choose to give us your email we keep it so we can follow up. Details below, in plain language.

Your scouting data never leaves your device

Opponents, imported snaps, formations, situations, defensive calls, playbook diagrams and self-scout data are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see them, and we have no way to retrieve them.

If you clear your browser data, that information is gone — use the in-app Export Backup to keep your own copy.

Files you import — Hudl playlist spreadsheets and scouting PDFs — are read entirely inside your browser. The file itself is never uploaded to us or to a third party.

Defensive calls in an imported file. A Hudl playlist can contain the coverage and pressure calls of whichever staff filmed the game. DefWise ignores those columns unless you explicitly tick the box confirming they are your own calls, and even then they are stored only on your device, like the rest of your data. We never receive them.

What we do collect

Product analytics. DefWise uses PostHog to understand how the app is used, so a solo developer can tell which features are worth keeping. This records things like pages viewed, which features you open (importing a file, running the predictor, printing a call sheet), and basic technical details your browser sends anyway — approximate location derived from your IP address, device type and browser. It sets a cookie and uses local storage to recognise a returning browser.

It does not capture your scouting content. Opponent names, formations, play names, snap data and defensive calls are never included in analytics.

Your email address, only if you give it. DefWise may ask for your email after you run the demo, and you can decline — the app works exactly the same either way. If you enter it, we store it (together with the Week 1 opponent you optionally name) so that we can email you, follow up on how the tool is working, or build the opponent breakdown you asked for. It is kept in PostHog and mirrored to a private spreadsheet only we can see. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone, and we do not add you to any list you cannot leave.

Other services we rely on

  • Software libraries (CDN): the app loads open-source PDF and spreadsheet readers from a content-delivery network so it can read your files. These requests are standard web traffic and never include your scouting data.
  • Hosting (Cloudflare): defwise.com is served through Cloudflare, which processes standard web-server request data such as IP addresses.
  • Payments (Gumroad): if you buy a Season Pass, payment is handled by Gumroad. We never see your card details. See Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • License verification: activating a Season Pass sends your license key to Gumroad to confirm the purchase. No scouting data is sent.
  • Feedback form: the Feedback button opens a Google Form. Anything you submit there is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Opting out, and deleting what we have

Analytics are blocked by most privacy extensions and browser tracking protection, and the app works normally without them. To remove everything stored locally, clear your browser's site data for defwise.com.

If you gave us your email and want it deleted, email hello@defwise.com and we will remove it from our analytics and our spreadsheet. No explanation needed.

Children's privacy

DefWise is a tool for coaches and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
