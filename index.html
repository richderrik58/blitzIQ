Export the Formation Tendencies report — not Run/Pass Breakdown
The Formation Tendencies report contains the formation names (TREY R WEAK, GUN TRIPS, etc.), usage fractions like 8/125, and run/pass splits BlitzIQ needs to build your scouting report.
How to export from your scouting platform:
1 Open your opponent's report and go to Tendencies
2 Select Formation from the tendency type dropdown
3 Click Export → PDF
4 Upload that PDF here — you can select multiple PDFs at once (formation report + down-distance report)
⚠ Run/Pass Breakdown PDFs
These only contain overall run/pass totals — no formation names. BlitzIQ can import the percentages but cannot build formation cards or generate defensive recommendations without a Formation Tendencies report.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  BlitzIQ Beta

We collect nothing.

BlitzIQ stores all data locally on your device using your browser's built-in storage (localStorage). No scouting data, formation names, play information, or usage data is ever sent to any server.

PDF files

When you import a scouting PDF, it is processed entirely within your browser. The file is never uploaded to any server or third party. It is read once, parsed for formation data, and the original file is discarded.

Feedback form

If you choose to send feedback, tapping the Feedback button opens a Google Form in a new tab. Any information you submit is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. BlitzIQ only receives what you voluntarily enter in that form.

No accounts, no tracking

BlitzIQ has no user accounts, no login, no cookies, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not know who uses the app or how often.

Data deletion

To delete all your data, clear your browser's site data for this page. All opponents, formations, and settings will be removed immediately.
