Export the Formation Tendencies report — not Run/Pass Breakdown
The Formation Tendencies report contains the formation names (TREY R WEAK, GUN TRIPS, etc.), usage fractions like 8/125, and run/pass splits DefWise needs to build your scouting report.
How to export from your scouting platform:
1 Open your opponent's report and go to Tendencies
2 Select Formation from the tendency type dropdown
3 Click Export → PDF
4 Upload that PDF here — you can select multiple PDFs at once (formation report + down-distance report)
⚠ Run/Pass Breakdown PDFs
These only contain overall run/pass totals — no formation names. DefWise can import the percentages but cannot build formation cards or generate defensive recommendations without a Formation Tendencies report.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  DefWise Beta

Your scouting data stays on your device.

Opponents, snaps, formations, defensive calls, playbook diagrams and self-scout data are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. We can't see them and can't retrieve them.

Files you import

Hudl playlists and scouting PDFs are read entirely inside your browser. The file is never uploaded anywhere. If a playlist contains a staff's coverage and pressure calls, DefWise ignores them unless you tick the box confirming they're your own — and even then they stay on your device.

What we do collect

DefWise uses PostHog for product analytics, so a solo developer can see which features actually get used. That records pages viewed, which features you open, and basic technical details your browser sends anyway (approximate location from your IP, device, browser). It sets a cookie. It never includes your scouting content — no opponent names, formations, play names or snap data.

Your email — only if you give it

We may ask for your email after the demo, and you can say no; the app works the same either way. If you enter it, we keep it (with the Week 1 opponent you optionally name) so we can follow up or build the breakdown you asked for. We never sell or share it. Want it gone? Email us and it's deleted, no explanation needed.

Feedback form

The Feedback button opens a Google Form in a new tab. Anything you submit there is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Opting out & deleting data

Analytics are blocked by most privacy extensions and the app works normally without them. To delete everything stored locally, clear your browser's site data for this page.
