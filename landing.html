You need a Hudl export (the playlist Excel or a scouting PDF) — the same files you already pull for an opponent. DefWise turns them into tendencies and predictions. If you can export it, DefWise can read it.

No. Keep breaking down film the way you do — DefWise never asks you to stop. It reads the export that comes out the other end, so what you charted is on paper and in the box, where film can't follow you.
